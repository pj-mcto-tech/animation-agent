Title: The Little Robot Who Learned to Paint
Author: Animation Agent Sample
Version: 1.0

In a sunlit workshop, a small blue robot named Bit picks up a paintbrush for the first time.
Bit dips the brush into a bucket of bright red paint and strokes it carefully across a blank canvas.
The robot steps back, tilts its head, and watches the wet paint glisten in the afternoon light.
Sparks of inspiration flicker across Bit's eyes as it reaches for a second color.
Bit paints a wide golden horizon, then a row of crooked green trees along the edge.
Finally, Bit signs the canvas with a cheerful beep and holds the finished painting toward the camera.
